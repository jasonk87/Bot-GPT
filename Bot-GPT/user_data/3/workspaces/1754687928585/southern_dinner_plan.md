### Weekly Southern Dinner Plan

**Days 1–3: BBQ Focus**
- **Monday**: Barbecue Chicken + Cornbread + Coleslaw
- **Tuesday**: Pulled Pork BBQ + Macaroni and Cheese + Biscuits
- **Wednesday**: Nashville Hot Chicken + Green Bean Casserole + Cornbread

**Days 4–5: Comfort Food**
- **Thursday**: BBQ Shrimp and Grits + Collard Greens
- **Friday**: Hot Water Cornbread + Fried Okra + Macaroni and Cheese

**Days 6–7: Dessert and Variety**
- **Saturday**: Old Southern Chocolate Pecan Cake + Collard Greens
- **Sunday**: BBQ Chicken Enchiladas + Side of Biscuits

### Key Tips
1. Keep cornmeal, buttermilk, pork, chicken, and BBQ sauce on hand.
2. Batch cook sides like macaroni and cheese.
3. Swap frozen veggies (e.g., okra, green beans) for variety.
4. Save this plan in your pantry for quick reference!
